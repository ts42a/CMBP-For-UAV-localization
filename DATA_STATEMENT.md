# Code and data rights

Reviewed 2026-10-14. The repository's MIT licence applies to original CMBL code
and documentation. It does not relicense datasets, satellite imagery, pretrained
weights, or third-party software. Their respective terms continue to apply.

## UAV-VisLoc

Obtain the dataset from its [official repository](https://github.com/IntelliSensing/UAV-VisLoc)
and cite Xu et al., *UAV-VisLoc: A Large-scale Dataset for UAV Visual Localization*,
2024, arXiv:2405.11936. The reviewed repository supplies download links and a
citation, but no explicit dataset redistribution licence was located there.
Availability for download is not treated as permission to redistribute it.
Keep raw imagery, crops, per-frame coordinates, and derived imagery caches local
or in access-controlled research storage; do not include them in public releases
without establishing the necessary rights. Private hosting alone does not settle
whether a third-party upload is permitted.

## Google Maps / Google Earth imagery

The dataset authors describe their satellite imagery as downloaded from Google
Maps. Any Google Earth material used separately is also subject to its applicable
terms. Review Google's [Geo Guidelines](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/geo-guidelines/)
and [Maps/Earth additional terms](https://www.google.com/intl/en-US_ML/help/terms_maps-earth/)
for the particular source and use. Research/publication permissions and attribution
requirements do not establish a general right to bulk download, redistribute
imagery, or train/test machine-learning systems. Retain Google and applicable
third-party attribution in permitted figures; CMBL's MIT notice cannot replace it.

This statement records release boundaries, not proof that every historical
download or upload has been licensed. Public release of the dataset, imagery,
coordinate tables or derived caches remains pending a source-specific rights check.
The repository can distribute original code, protocols and aggregate research
reports separately. DINOv2 source and weights retain their upstream terms.
